# Requirements Document (SEED, Claude Code draft: reference only, not fed to Kiro)

> **Seed status:** Claude Code draft (v0.1, lightly patched in v0.2). Per PRD Decisions log #2, the canonical `replay-viewer` spec is generated by Kiro from PRD §7.3 **without** this seed. This file is kept only for the "seed vs Kiro output" comparison. PRD §7.3 supersedes it (chase camera, gap ribbon, lead-change beats, committed style, per-segment trails, clip export moved to P2).

## Introduction

The Replay Viewer is the heart of the demo. It shows the shared stretch on a dark map and animates the present rider as an ember-orange trail and the ghost (the past self) as a glowing, translucent ectoplasm trail, both on one shared clock. The user controls playback with a scrubber and speed controls. A Gap_HUD reports the live time gap, and a gap-by-distance chart with an elevation profile sits under the scrubber. The aesthetic is "haunted, but legible": it respects reduced motion, meets WCAG AA contrast and never relies on colour alone.

## Glossary

- **Replay_Screen**: The `/ride` page state after alignment. It contains the map, HUD, scrubber and chart.
- **Map_View**: The MapLibre GL map with an interleaved deck.gl `MapboxOverlay`.
- **Ghost_Layer**: Two stacked deck.gl `TripsLayer`s (glow and core) for each ghost.
- **Present_Layer**: The `TripsLayer` plus head marker for the present rider.
- **Playback_Clock**: A zustand store that holds replay time τ, the play state and the speed. It is advanced by `requestAnimationFrame`.
- **Scrubber**: A range control that maps position f ∈ [0, 1] to τ.
- **Gap_HUD**: The top overlay showing the ahead/behind state, the time gap, the distance gap and the remaining shared distance.
- **Gap_Chart**: A chart of gapS(d) over route distance, with the present ride's elevation profile and rider markers.
- **Spook_Settings**: User preferences: "Less spooky", sound on/off and Clock_Mode. Stored in localStorage.
- **Run_Picker**: The list of detected Overlap_Runs that the user chooses from.
- **Clip_Exporter**: (P1) Records a short vertical video of the replay in the browser.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Map and attribution

**User Story:** As a rider, I want the replay on a real map, so that I recognise the road.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Map_View SHALL render the committed, pre-recoloured style `public/map/haunted-style.json` (OpenFreeMap tiles, glyphs and sprites) and SHALL load client-side only (`next/dynamic` with `ssr: false`).
2. THE Map_View SHALL always display the basemap attribution ("OpenFreeMap © OpenMapTiles Data from OpenStreetMap"), unobscured by the HUD at every supported viewport.
3. WHEN an Alignment loads, THE Map_View SHALL show a full-route overview (fit to the Route_Axis with 48 px padding), then hand over to the chase camera (Requirement 12); it SHALL return to the overview when playback finishes.
4. THE Map_View SHALL draw the full present ride dimmed (opacity ≤ 0.25) and the shared stretch at full strength.

### Requirement 2: Ghost and present trails

**User Story:** As a rider, I want to see my past self as a ghost racing me, so that the comparison feels alive.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Ghost_Layer SHALL render each ghost as two `TripsLayer`s: a wide glow (width ≥ 10 px, alpha ≈ 0.25) and a thin core (width ≈ 3 px, alpha ≈ 0.9) in the ghost's token colour, with `fadeTrail` enabled.
2. THE Present_Layer SHALL render the present rider in `--ember` with a head marker.
3. THE Ghost_Layer and Present_Layer SHALL use timestamps on the shared replay clock (re-zeroed seconds for the active Clock_Mode). They SHALL draw the ghost along the Route_Axis using `ghostPositionAt`, and SHALL emit one path per segment so that no trail crosses a Segment_Break.
4. WHILE playing, THE Map_View SHALL update layers via `overlay.setProps` from a single `requestAnimationFrame` loop. React state SHALL update at most 10 times per second.

### Requirement 3: Playback controls

**User Story:** As a rider, I want to play, pause, speed up and scrub, so that I can jump to the part of the road I care about.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Replay_Screen SHALL provide play/pause and speed options 1×, 4×, 16× and 64×. The default SHALL be 16×.
2. THE Scrubber SHALL map position f ∈ [0, 1] linearly to τ ∈ [0, τ_max], where τ_max is the larger of the two riders' total times on the run.
3. WHEN the user drags or clicks the Scrubber, THE Playback_Clock SHALL seek to the corresponding τ within one frame.
4. THE Scrubber SHALL be keyboard operable: Space toggles play, ←/→ step 1% of τ_max, Shift+←/→ step 10%, and Home/End seek to the start/end. It SHALL expose `aria-valuetext` such as "2.4 km of 5.1, behind by 12 seconds".
5. WHEN τ reaches τ_max, THE Playback_Clock SHALL pause and SHALL emit a `finished` event.

### Requirement 4: Gap HUD

**User Story:** As a rider, I want to know instantly whether I'm ahead or behind, so that the race has tension.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Gap_HUD SHALL display the state as word + arrow + signed time in tabular mono digits, e.g. `AHEAD ▲ +0:08` or `BEHIND ▼ −0:12`. When |gap| < 0.5 s it SHALL display `LEVEL ●`.
2. THE Gap_HUD SHALL derive the time gap from `gapAtD(D_R(τ))` and SHALL show the distance gap in metres and the shared distance remaining.
3. THE Gap_HUD SHALL never use colour as the only indicator of ahead or behind.
4. WHEN the lead changes, THE Gap_HUD SHALL play a brief flicker (≤ 300 ms). WHILE reduced motion or "Less spooky" is active, it SHALL show no flicker.

### Requirement 5: Gap chart and elevation

**User Story:** As a rider, I want a chart of where the gap opened, so that I can see the story of the race at a glance.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Gap_Chart SHALL plot gapS(d) over route distance in kilometres, with "behind" below the zero line and "ahead" above it, consistent with the HUD.
2. THE Gap_Chart SHALL show the present ride's elevation profile beneath the gap line and SHALL mark detected climbs.
3. THE Gap_Chart SHALL show both riders' current positions as markers synced to the Playback_Clock. Clicking the chart SHALL seek to that distance.
4. THE Gap_Chart SHALL render with SVG or canvas inside the app, without a heavyweight chart library unless bundle size remains under 60 kB gzip for the chart.

### Requirement 6: Run selection and clock mode

**User Story:** As a rider whose rides overlapped in several places, I want to choose which shared stretch to race, so that I compare the part I care about.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN alignment returns more than one Overlap_Run, THE Run_Picker SHALL list each one with its length and coverage, preselecting the longest.
2. IF alignment returns no Overlap_Run, THEN THE Replay_Screen SHALL show the empty state "These rides never crossed paths." with actions to swap files or to load the sample ghosts.
3. THE Replay_Screen SHALL offer a "Moving time / Elapsed time" toggle. The default SHALL be moving time.

### Requirement 7: Motion, sound and accessibility

**User Story:** As a user sensitive to motion or flashing, I want a calmer mode, so that the app is comfortable to use.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHILE `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is set OR "Less spooky" is enabled, THE Replay_Screen SHALL disable fog drift and flicker and SHALL keep the trails.
2. THE Replay_Screen SHALL keep all text at a contrast ratio ≥ 4.5:1 against its immediate background.
3. THE Replay_Screen SHALL start with sound muted. Ambient audio SHALL play only after a user gesture and an explicit unmute.
4. THE Replay_Screen SHALL make every control reachable and operable by keyboard, with visible focus rings.

### Requirement 8: Responsive layout and performance

**User Story:** As a judge on a phone, I want the replay to work on a small screen, so that I can try it anywhere.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Replay_Screen SHALL lay out without horizontal scrolling from 375 px to 1920 px wide: HUD at the top, map in the middle, scrubber and chart at the bottom.
2. THE Replay_Screen SHALL keep touch targets at least 44 × 44 px.
3. WHILE playing a 10,000-point alignment, THE Map_View SHALL sustain ≥ 50 fps on a 2022-class laptop. Per-frame JavaScript work SHALL stay ≤ 4 ms. Display polylines SHALL be simplified separately from the analysis data.

### Requirement 9: Sample quick-start

**User Story:** As a judge, I want the sample replay to start by itself, so that I see the product in seconds.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Replay_Screen is opened via `/ride?sample=1`, THE Replay_Screen SHALL load the sample ghosts, select the longest run and auto-play at 16× within 20 s on a typical broadband connection.

### Requirement 10: Séance view (P1)

**User Story:** As a rider, I want to see up to three past selves at once, so that I can watch my progression.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHERE several ghosts are aligned, THE Replay_Screen SHALL render each in its own token colour (`--ectoplasm`, `--wisp`, `--candle`), with one HUD row per ghost labelled with "N days ago".

### Requirement 11: Clip export (moved to P2 in v0.2; not planned)

The demo video is a screen recording; in-browser clip export is out of scope for the hackathon.

### Requirement 12: Chase camera, gap ribbon and lead-change beats (added v0.2)

**User Story:** As a viewer, I want to see the duel up close, so that a 12-second gap on a climb is visible rather than two overlapping dots.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHILE playing and reduced motion is off, THE Map_View SHALL follow the midpoint of both riders' heads with pitch ≈ 50°, an eased bearing along the route, and a zoom at which the head-to-head distance spans about 30% of the viewport width (clamped zoom range).
2. THE Map_View SHALL draw a Gap_Ribbon along the route between the two heads, labelled with the time gap in seconds and a word naming the leader.
3. WHEN the leader changes, THE Replay_Screen SHALL ease playback to 1× for about 2 s, flare the ghost and show an in-world caption; WHILE reduced motion or "Less spooky" is active, only the caption SHALL appear.
