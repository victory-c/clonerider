## D0. Product name: decided — **CloneRider** (Nov 16 2026)

The builder chose **CloneRider**: the app clones your past rides into riders you race. Rejected options: the Marvel comic-character name (trademark), "Ghostwriter" (an existing software trademark in the same classes; it also suggests writing on someone's behalf), and the earlier working names Doppelpace and Wraithline.
- A web search on Nov 16 found no app, product or trademark called CloneRider (just one small social-media hashtag). **Still to do before publishing:** a USPTO TESS/TSDR search (Classes 9, 28, 42) plus App Store and Play searches (Q1).
- The spooky theme stays in the in-product vocabulary (§1): past rides are still called *ghosts*, the narrator is *the Whisper*.

Fallback if the trademark search fails: **Wraithline**. Rules that hold whatever the name:
- Never use the Marvel-trademarked comic character name that the original nickname came from, anywhere in the product, repo, video or hashtags.
- No `Ghost___` prefix: Ghost-Bikes GmbH holds GHOST marks for bicycles (Class 12), and a GHOST CYCLING mark exists.
- Names already taken by apps or past winners, and avoided: Drafto, Ghost Trax, Ghostracer, Ghostlap, Banshee, Spectre, Poltergeist, Phantom, Seance (as a brand).

## 0. Summary for judges

- **What:** a browser app. Drop in today's ride and your archive of past rides (a folder or a bulk-export ZIP of `.FIT` / `.GPX` files). It finds **every past ride that shared road with today's**, ranks them by shared length, aligns each one by distance, and replays your past self as a glowing ghost beside you on a chase camera. A gap ribbon and HUD show ahead/behind. A spoken narrator in character explains where the time went, and a **Receipts** panel proves every number. Finally, **"Take the ghost home"** writes the shared stretch plus your ghost's timing as a binary `.FIT` course file, so the past self becomes a live pacer on your bike computer next ride.
- **Why Frankenstein:** it is a round trip through worlds that don't normally touch:
  **device** (binary .FIT protocol, decoded) → **browser** (Web Worker + computational geometry that finds shared road across hundreds of rides) → **WebGL** (MapLibre + deck.gl chase-cam replay) → **language model** (slot-rendered narration whose numbers *and* directions are grounded by construction) → **voice** (neural speech or browser speech) → **back to the device** (binary .FIT encode of a course your ghost will race on).
- **Why it matters:** riders repeat roads, but rarely whole routes. Strava's Matched Activities matches only *whole* activities with the same start, end, direction and distance ([Strava help](https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/216918597-Matched-Runs)). Segments need to be defined in advance. On-device ghosts are live and same-course only. CloneRider finds *partial* overlaps automatically, after the fact, across your whole archive, with no account, and your files never leave the browser.
- **Kiro workflow (planned; seeded outside Kiro, generated, refined and executed in Kiro):** 5 specs generated in Kiro from this PRD, with EARS requirements and property-based correctness properties; at least one Bugfix spec; steering (always / fileMatch / manual); command hooks that run property tests and grounding evals; the custom `ghost-lab` MCP server that Kiro uses to check its own alignment on real rides; that MCP plus the geo steering and PBT hook packaged as a **Kiro Power**; one custom agent (`geo-reviewer`) run by Kiro CLI on every PR. See §14.

## 2. Problem and audience

### 2.1 Problem
Endurance athletes ride the same roads again and again: the local climb, the commute, the Saturday loop. They want to know: **"Am I faster than I was, and *where* on the road did I gain or lose time?"** Today's tools answer only narrow versions of that question:

| Need | Today's answer | Shortfall |
|---|---|---|
| Compare rides that share only part of the route (same climb, different loops) | Segments (Strava, Garmin); VeloViewer and GoldenCheetah segment analysis | The segment must be *pre-defined*, and you mostly see its total time |
| Compare repeats of the same route over time | Strava **Matched Activities** | Whole-route only: start, end, direction and distance must all match, so a different loop that shares the climb is invisible ([Strava help](https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/216918597-Matched-Runs)) |
| See *where* along the road the gap opened | Live on-device "race an activity" / Virtual Partner; Strava Live Segments | Must be planned before the ride, on an identical course or a pre-made segment; no replay afterwards |
| Watch rides side by side | Strava **Flyby** (multi-activity map replay) | Aligned by clock time, not by distance along a shared road, so riders who started the stretch at different times aren't racing each other |
| Understand why | Paid AI ride summaries | Criticised for summaries that contradict the data; Cycling Weekly reported an AI summary saying a rider "crushed his editor in the sprint" when he was beaten ([Cycling Weekly](https://cyclingweekly.com/news/strava-says-its-new-ai-feature-is-not-a-novelty-but-i-think-its-pointless)) |

### 2.2 Evidence of demand (each claim linked)
- Device and indoor vendors ship self-racing: Garmin Virtual Partner / Race an Activity, Zwift HoloReplay, MyWhoosh Shadow Replay, Apple Race Route (watchOS 9). All are live and same-course, or indoor only.
- Hammerhead Karoo users request a ghost of a previous ride ([feature request](https://support.hammerhead.io/hc/en-us/community/posts/54181194360347/comments/54203413749403), ["Ride against ride"](https://support.hammerhead.io/hc/en-us/community/posts/26759404696987-Ride-Against-ride)). A community reply there suggests that a **post-ride** comparison is the realistic version, which is what we build.
- Zwift riders ask to race ghosts of past rides ([forum](https://forums.zwift.com/t/race-your-ghost-from-past-rides-or-at-least-sprint-segments/588260)); intervals.icu users ask for matched-activity comparison ([forum](https://forum.intervals.icu/t/matched-runs-similar-to-strava-feature-request/85420)); Garmin Connect users complain they can't compare repeated activities ([forum](https://forums.garmin.com/apps-software/mobile-apps-web/f/garmin-connect-web/337647/frustrating-that-gc-can-t-do-basic-things-like-compare-the-same-activities)).
- **Primary evidence to collect (Nov 22–25):** a 5-rider test with members of a university cycling club or tri team, on their own files. We record 2–3 real quotes and the moment each rider "got it", and put them in the README. **These replace the invented persona from v0.1.**

### 2.3 Audience
- **Primary:** amateur road cyclists and triathletes who own a GPS head unit or watch and repeat local roads. Every major device exports FIT or GPX.
- **Secondary:** runners (the algorithm is sport-agnostic; P2 toggle).
- **Demo audience:** hackathon judges with no files of their own. They get a bundled **sample archive** and one click.

## 3. Differentiation

| Capability | Device "race activity" / Live Segments | Strava Matched Activities | Strava Flyby | Segment tools (VeloViewer, GoldenCheetah) | Indie ghost apps (Drafto, Ghost Trax) | Open-source compare (TourGaze, MyTourBook) | **CloneRider** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Works post-ride on uploads | no | yes (inside Strava) | yes | yes | partial | yes | **yes** |
| Finds a *partial* overlap between different routes, automatically | no | no (whole route) | no | only predefined segments | no | same-route-ish | **yes, multi-run** |
| Searches your whole archive for rides sharing road with this one | no | same-route only | no | per segment | no | some | **yes (Haunted Archive)** |
| Distance-aligned gap curve (where you gained/lost) | live number | trend of totals | no (clock-aligned) | per segment | live | yes | **yes + chart + chase cam** |
| Narration grounded in computed numbers and directions | no | no | no | no | no | no | **yes, by construction (slots)** |
| Exports the ghost back to the device as a course | n/a | no | no | no | no | no | **yes (.FIT course)** |
| No account, files stay on device | n/a | no | no | no / desktop | no | desktop install | **yes** |

**Honesty note:** we do **not** claim to be the first ghost-racing app or the first overlap matcher. TourGaze and others match overlaps; segment tools are mature. Our claim is the *combination*: automatic partial-overlap discovery across an archive, distance-aligned replay, a narrator that cannot state a wrong number or a wrong direction, private browser processing, and the round trip back to the device.

**Pitch line:** *"Drop in your rides. CloneRider finds every time you've ridden this road before, even on a different route, and lets you race those ghosts. A narrator tells you where you won or lost time, and it can't get a number wrong. Then you take the ghost home to your bike computer."*

## 4. Goals and non-goals

### Goals (for the hackathon)
- **G1:** a judge goes from landing page to a narrated ghost replay in **≤ 3 clicks and ≤ 20 s**, using the sample archive.
- **G2:** for two real rides sharing ≥ 500 m of road, alignment finds the shared stretch and the gap curve with **no manual input**. If the Nov 20 kill criterion fails, a manual "pick start and end" fallback ships instead (§16).
- **G3:** **zero ungrounded numbers and zero wrong-direction claims** in narration, guaranteed by slot rendering and enforced by property tests.
- **G4:** a polished, accessible, coherent UI on desktop and on a phone, specified in its own Kiro spec (`haunted-ui`), not improvised late.
- **G5:** Kiro usage that does real work and leaves artifacts: specs generated in Kiro, PBT, steering, command hooks, the `ghost-lab` MCP, a Kiro Power, one custom agent, the CLI in CI.

### Non-goals
- Live, during-ride racing in our own app (the device does that with our exported course).
- User accounts, social feeds, leaderboards, comparing against *other* athletes.
- Garmin Connect API or any unofficial scraping (API approval is business-only; scraping breaks the ToS).
- Storing rides on a server.
- Training advice or medical claims.
- **Strava API integration.** Dropped from the plan entirely (single-player mode means judges can't use it; terms restrict display and AI use). Strava *bulk-export ZIPs* that the user downloads themselves are just files and are supported by the Haunted Archive.
- TCX (P2 only; devices export FIT and most apps export GPX).
- In-browser video clip export (P2 / dropped; the demo video is a screen recording).

## 6. User journeys

### J1: Judge quick-start (P0, the most important path)
1. The judge opens the URL. Landing: a dark long-exposure map with ghost light-trails, and the button **"Summon the sample ghosts"**.
2. One click loads the bundled, privacy-scrubbed sample (a present ride plus past rides that share a climb).
3. Replay opens: full-route overview → the chase camera swoops to the two riders → the ghost pulls ahead on the climb, the gap ribbon stretches, and the HUD shows `BEHIND ▼ −0:12`. A lead change gets a slow-motion beat.
4. At the finish the camera pulls back to the overview; the Whisper speaks 3–6 lines with captions.
5. The judge opens **Receipts**: every number links to the fact it came from.
6. P1: "The archive found 6 ghosts of you on this climb" → Séance or "Take the ghost home".

### J2: Own rides (P0)
1. Drag in 2+ `.fit` / `.gpx` files. A badge says "Parsed on your device: nothing uploaded".
2. Optional one-click privacy: **"Hide around this ride's start"** (+ radius slider), plus saved zones. Trimming happens before anything renders.
3. The app lists the shared stretches (length, % of each ride), preselecting the longest.
4. Replay, scrub, play/pause, gap chart, narration, Receipts.

### J3: Haunted Archive (P1, the headline P1)
1. Drop a **folder** (or, stretch, a bulk-export ZIP) of 50–500 past rides, then pick today's ride.
2. The worker indexes the archive and answers **"which past rides share road with this one?"**, ranked by shared length, with a count: "14 ghosts of you on this climb".
3. Pick one ghost to race (J2 flow) or up to 3 for the Séance.

### J4: Take the ghost home (P1)
- On any replay: **"Take the ghost home"** downloads a `.FIT` course of the shared stretch, with the ghost's timing in its records. Loaded on a bike computer that supports course pacing, the past self becomes a live pacer. The help text stays device-agnostic and lists no brands.

### J5: Séance (P1, lowest)
- Up to 3 ghosts against one present ride, pairwise axes only, each with its own hue and HUD row.

### 7.2 Ghost alignment (spec `ghost-alignment`)
| ID | Req | Pri |
|---|---|---|
| A-1 | Resample each **segment** by distance (Δ = 5 m; 10 m for rides > 50 km). Spacing is exact on the distance parameter; never interpolate across a segment break | P0 |
| A-2 | Smoothed heading: bearing of a 40 m chord (`HEADING_CHORD_M`) centred on each point, never from consecutive 5 m points | P0 |
| A-3 | Corridor matching: Flatbush index of the present ride's segments; for each ghost point, among candidate segments within r = 25 m **whose heading differs by < 60°**, choose the smallest perpendicular distance; while a run is active, prefer candidates inside the forward window. Project on the 2-point segment: `s_R = distM_R[j] + t · segLen_j` | P0 |
| A-4 | Overlap runs: monotone in `s_R` (−5 m tolerance), unmatched gaps ≤ 75 m bridged (never across a segment break), minimum 500 m. On a forward-window miss, query globally at once; a heading-valid match more than 5 m behind `s_prev` closes the run and opens a new one (laps), with no hysteresis wait. All runs returned, ranked by length, with present and ghost index ranges | P0 |
| A-5 | Reverse-direction passes produce no overlap | P0 |
| A-6 | Shared route axis = the present polyline over the run. Present: `d = distM_R[i] − startRefM` over its index range. Ghost: `d = s_R − startRefM` from the matcher, interpolated by ghost distance across bridged gaps, then running max. `T(d)` is a lower-bound search ("first time d is reached") | P0 |
| A-7 | Gap curves `gapS(d) = T_present(d) − T_ghost(d)` (positive = present is behind); ghost drawn on the present line at equal replay time; moving-time default, elapsed toggle | P0 |
| A-8 | Performance: 10k × 10k points aligned in ≤ 150 ms in the worker on a 2022 laptop | P0 |
| A-9 | Climb detection on the shared axis (≥ 300 m, ≥ 3% average grade) | P0 |
| A-10 | **Manual fallback** (ships only if the Nov 20 kill criterion fails): the user picks a start and end on the present ride; matching runs only inside that range | P0-conditional |
| A-11 | Archive search: bbox prefilter, then the same corridor matcher per candidate; returns ghosts ranked by shared length; 200 rides searched in ≤ 5 s in the worker | P1 |
| A-12 | Séance: up to 3 ghosts, **pairwise axes only** | P1 |
| — | Known limitation (documented, P2 fix): on a closed-loop present ride, a shared stretch that crosses the start/finish seam splits into two runs | — |

## 8. Ghost alignment algorithm (design level)

```
files ─► parse+clean (Worker) ─► privacy trim (segment breaks) ─► resample per segment (Δ) ─► 40 m chord headings
      ─► corridor match (Flatbush, per-segment projection, heading-gated candidate choice)
      ─► monotone runs (hysteresis, lap restart, never across breaks) ─► route axis (present = own distM, ghost = s_R)
      ─► T(d), D(τ), gapS(d) ─► replay  /  RideFacts ─► narrator  /  .FIT course ─► device
```

1. **Normalise.** Typed arrays; GPS distance recomputed; pauses flagged; positions frozen while stopped; `segStart` marks breaks. Two time axes: `elapsed` and `moving`.
2. **Resample by distance, per segment.** Every channel is linearly interpolated on cumulative distance at step Δ. The resampled `distM` values are exactly `kΔ` (plus the exact segment end). Geometric polyline length is ≤ the original (resampling cuts corners); that is expected and tested as such.
3. **Headings** are bearings of a 40 m chord (distM − 20 m → distM + 20 m), so 3–5 m GPS noise doesn't scramble them.
4. **Corridor matching.** R = present, G = ghost. Index R's segments in Flatbush (bbox padded by 25 m). For each G point: candidates within 25 m, keep those with heading difference < 60°, choose the smallest perpendicular distance (prefer the forward window `[s_prev − 50, s_prev + 500]` while a run is active). Project on the 2-point segment for `s_R`. Cost O(n log m).
5. **Runs.** Extend while matched and `s_R` is non-decreasing (−5 m tolerance); bridge unmatched gaps ≤ 75 m; never bridge a segment break. On a forward-window miss, query globally at once; a valid match far behind `s_prev` starts a new run (the next lap). Keep runs ≥ 500 m; rank by length.
6. **Route axis and gap.** The present polyline over the run *is* the axis, so the present needs no projection. The ghost's `d` comes from `s_R`. Running max, re-zero time at entry, `T(d)` by lower-bound search, `gapS(d) = T_R(d) − T_G(d)`. Every frame is O(log n).

**Rejected:** discrete Fréchet and full DTW (O(n·m), 1e8 cells), `turf.nearestPointOnLine` in a loop (O(n·m)), `turf.lineOverlap` (brittle). Turf is a test oracle only.

**Correctness is defined by properties** (fast-check), written in the Kiro-generated `design.md`. The seed list is in `docs/seed-specs/ghost-alignment/design.md`: identity, antisymmetry, time-shift, time scaling, resampling bounds (on the distance parameter), monotonicity, correlated-noise robustness, extension invariance, direction rejection (for tracks without opposite-direction self-overlap), sampling-rate invariance (low curvature), pause handling, ghost-on-route, lap separation (laps ≥ 2 km), break safety (no point or vertex inside a privacy zone).

## 9. AI narrator grounding: correct by construction

1. **Facts only.** The model receives a zod-validated `RideFacts` JSON (ids, labels, kinds, values, signs) and nothing else about the rides. It sees the values so it can choose the story (the biggest swing, the decisive climb), but it cannot copy them into the text: any digit outside a slot is rejected.
2. **The model writes templates, not numbers.** Output: `{ lines: [{ text }] }`, where numbers, ordinals, relative dates and **directions** can only appear as slots: `{finish.gap}`, `{finish.gap:ahead}`, `{climb2.ordinal}`, `{daysAgoPhrase}`.
3. **Rendering is deterministic.** Numbers come from `fact.display`; direction words come from `sign(fact.value)` with the convention `gapS > 0 ⇒ present behind ⇒ the ghost ("I") is ahead`.
4. **Validation is simple and complete.** Unknown slot → reject. Direction slot on an unsigned fact → reject. Any digit, number word, ordinal or polarity word in the raw text outside a slot → reject (allowlist: "one" as a pronoun). This closes the "you beat me by 12 seconds when you lost" hole, because the model never chooses the direction word.
5. **Failure policy.** Retry once with the error list, then the template narrator (always valid; a property test proves it).
6. **Tone** (system prompt + `narrator-voice.md`): second person, playful-eerie, never cruel, no health/training prescriptions, no causal claims (weather, fatigue) unless a fact states them.
7. **Receipts** highlight the rendered slot spans, so there is no fragile re-parsing of free text.

## 10. Data privacy

- **Client-side parsing.** Files are read with `File.arrayBuffer()` and parsed in a Web Worker. The demo shows this with an "airplane mode" shot: the network is turned off and the app still parses, aligns and replays.
- **Privacy trimming** runs in the worker before anything is rendered, exported or sent: one-click start zone, saved zones (default 300 m) and a fixed 200 m endpoint trim. Trimming creates segment breaks; the resampler, matcher, renderer and FIT exporter never cross a break.
- **What leaves the device.** Only the `RideFacts` JSON (fact ids, labels and computed numbers; no coordinates, place names or dates), and only when narration is requested. Neural TTS (opt-in) sends only the rendered narration text.
- **No storage by default.** Rides live in memory; zones and settings in localStorage under the `STORAGE_PREFIX`; IndexedDB is opt-in (P2).
- **Server logs** never include request bodies.
- **Committed fixtures and samples** are scrubbed with the trimmer **and** tested: no point within 1 km of home/work (coordinates from a gitignored env file).
- **Analytics:** none, or cookieless page views only.

## 13. Success metrics and demo acceptance criteria

**Product metrics (on the builder's real rides):**
- Overlap detection on 3+ hand-labelled real pairs: precision ≥ 95%, recall ≥ 90% (**kill criterion: < 90% precision on Nov 20 → manual fallback A-10**).
- Time-gap error ≤ 2 s at the end of the shared stretch vs. hand-computed gaps.
- Narration: **0** ungrounded numbers and **0** wrong-direction claims in 50 generations (guaranteed by rendering; `pnpm narrator:eval` measures the template-fallback rate).
- Performance: parse a 3 MB FIT ≤ 400 ms; align 10k × 10k ≤ 150 ms; archive search 200 rides ≤ 5 s (P1); replay ≥ 50 fps on a 2022 laptop, ≥ 30 fps on a mid-range phone.
- Lighthouse on the landing page: Accessibility ≥ 95, Performance ≥ 80.
- **5-rider test:** at least 4 of 5 riders find a shared stretch on their own files without help.

**Demo acceptance criteria (production URL, incognito, before recording):**
1. Landing → "Summon the sample ghosts" → replay playing in ≤ 20 s.
2. Overview → chase camera; the gap ribbon is readable at 1080p and at 375 px.
3. The lead changes at least once, with the slow-motion beat.
4. Narration plays (pre-generated audio or browser voice) with captions; every number and direction is clickable in Receipts.
5. Two real FIT files work **with the network turned off** (airplane-mode shot).
6. "Hide around this ride's start" visibly trims the start.
7. "Less spooky" disables camera motion, flicker and fog.
8. Works at 375 × 812.
9. Attribution visible; no third-party logos.
10. (P1) The archive finds ≥ 5 ghosts in the sample archive; (P1) the exported `.FIT` course round-trips through our parser.
